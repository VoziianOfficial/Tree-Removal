Overview

Finish Ground

Grinding reduces the visible stump into wood chips so the surface can be leveled, replanted, mulched, or prepared for another project. The right plan depends on stump size, depth expectations, and machine access.

  • Clarify grinding depth and whether exposed roots are included.
  • Decide whether chips should be removed, spread, or left for backfill.
  • Confirm access width, slope, gates, nearby hardscape, and irrigation risks.

Benefits

Usable Ground

A clear grinding scope prevents a common mismatch: a stump may be gone visually, but the remaining chips, root flare, and soil level determine whether the yard feels complete.

Questions

Grinding FAQ

Depth depends on the intended finish. Lawn repair, planting, and construction preparation can require different grinding expectations.
