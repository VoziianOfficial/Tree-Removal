Privacy Overview

TimberRoot is an independent tree service aggregator website. This policy explains what information may be collected when you browse the site, submit a project inquiry, or contact us about tree removal, stump grinding, storm cleanup, or related outdoor work.

We keep the information we ask for focused on responding to requests and maintaining the website. Please avoid sending sensitive personal, financial, medical, or government-identification information through the contact form.

Data Collected

When you submit a request, we may collect your name, email address, selected service, property or project details you choose to include, and the message you provide. This helps us understand the scope of your inquiry and route the conversation appropriately.

  • Contact details, such as name and email address.
  • Service preferences, such as tree removal or stump grinding.
  • Project notes, photos, scheduling constraints, access concerns, or location details that you voluntarily include.
  • Basic technical information that browsers and servers commonly exchange, such as device type, approximate region, page interactions, and security logs.

Data Use

Information is used to process contact requests, improve site content, maintain security, prevent abuse, and communicate with you about tree removal or stump grinding inquiries. We may also use aggregated, non-identifying information to understand which pages and services are most useful to visitors.

Submitting a request does not create a service contract. Any final scheduling, site inspection, permit review, pricing, insurance confirmation, or work agreement should be handled directly with the provider you choose.

Third Parties

TimberRoot is an independent aggregator. If you choose to work with a provider, confirm that provider's credentials, insurance, privacy practices, availability, and final estimate directly before authorizing work.

We do not sell contact form submissions. Information may be shared only when needed to respond to your inquiry, comply with law, protect the website, or support normal technical operations such as hosting, email delivery, analytics, spam prevention, and security monitoring.

Local Storage

The cookie notice stores your acceptance in your browser local storage so the compact notice does not keep reappearing. This value is stored on your device and does not include your name, email address, selected service, or project message.

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards for information handled through the website. No internet transmission or storage method can be guaranteed completely secure, so please keep form submissions limited to information needed for the inquiry.

Your Choices

You can choose not to submit the form, clear local storage in your browser, or contact us to ask about information you previously provided. Browser settings may also allow you to block, delete, or limit local site data.

If your request has already been referred to or discussed with a third-party provider, you may need to contact that provider directly for changes, deletions, or updates related to their own records.
